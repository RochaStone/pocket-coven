# Founder World reel kit: handover

A 18 to 20 second, 9:16 Instagram reel introducing Rocha (the founder) as an animated Merwitch,
walking through her founder-world framework: Brand, AI Architecture, Energetics, Belonging.
Thriving Era Studio. Voiceover by Rocha.

## Start here

1. Run `./get-frames.sh` to download the seven painted frames into `frames/` (752 x 1344 JPEG).
2. Read `REEL-SCRIPT.md`: beat timings, which frame and overlays go on each beat, motion ideas,
   the voiceover script, type notes and the Instagram caption.
3. Rocha records the voiceover first. Cut the frames to her pauses, not to fixed timings.

## What's in the folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `REEL-SCRIPT.md` | The edit sheet: the source of truth for order, timing and overlays |
| `get-frames.sh` | Downloads the painted frames |
| `card-1-brief.png`, `card-2-dashboard.png`, `card-3-automation.png` | Flashcards for beat 7, transparent, 1080 x 1920 |
| `title-01` to `title-04` | Room labels (BRAND, AI ARCHITECTURE, ENERGETICS, BELONGING), beats 3 to 6 |
| `title-05` to `title-09` | Accent words (experience, architecture, pulse, belong, genius), beats 3 to 6 and 8 |
| `title-10-end-card.png` | End card, beat 9 |
| `SAMPLE-titles-on-frame.jpg` | Shows how a label and accent word sit on a frame |

Every overlay PNG is full frame (1080 x 1920) with a transparent background and is already positioned,
so lay it straight over its frame with no repositioning.

## Notes

- Frames are 752 x 1344; scale to fill 1080 x 1920 with a slow push-in to hide the upscale.
- Keep the bottom 20% clear of anything important for Instagram's buttons, apart from the accent words,
  which sit just above that zone.
- Flashcards are illustrative (invented content, no product or client names). Do not label them as real results.
- Turn on Instagram auto captions for the voiceover.
