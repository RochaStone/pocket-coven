# Founder World intro reel: edit sheet

18 to 20 seconds, 9:16 (1080 x 1920). The last frame loops straight back into the first.

Record the voiceover first, then cut the frames to its pauses. Turn on Instagram's auto captions.
With the voiceover carrying the words, the screen only needs the room labels, one accent word per beat
(in the italic serif) and the end card. The full lines in the table below are the spoken script.

## Voiceover

What if you could step inside a founder's world?
This one's mine. I build them.
Where brand becomes an experience.
AI becomes architecture.
And your essence sets the pulse.
A world your people want to belong to.
Built to run without you.
So you live in your genius.
Welcome to your founder world.

## Type

- Accent words: Cormorant Garamond Italic (experience, architecture, pulse, belong, genius).
- Labels: Fredoka, small widely letter-spaced capitals in a champagne-gold pill, top third, same spot every time.
- Any bigger titles: Fredoka Bold, white with a deep plum outline and soft pink glow.
- End card: Thriving Era Studio brand fonts.
